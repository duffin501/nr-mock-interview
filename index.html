NR MOCK INTERVIEWHampton Roads NROTC Nuclear Prep

Timed technical interview

You get one problem from the unit's NR practice bank (about 1,000 problems built from the NSTC practice packet and the qual card) and 10 minutes to work it out loud on camera. Your run is graded and the report goes to you, LT Cole and LT Catozzi.

Pick a subject only to drill a weak area. The real interview does not let you choose.

Rules

  • 10 minutes, hard stop. The clock starts the moment the problem appears. At 10:00 the interview ends whether you are done or not.
  • The recording does not pause. It starts before the problem appears and runs until you finish. There is no pause button. If you leave this tab or the camera drops, the time is logged and shows in your report.
  • Allowed: paper or a whiteboard, pencil or marker, and a scientific calculator no more advanced than a TI-36X Pro. No graphing calculators.
  • Not allowed: notes, books, your phone, other tabs, or anyone helping.
  • Talk the whole time, like the real interview. Name the principle, draw the picture, state your assumptions, check units, and say your final answer out loud and box it.
  • At the end you hold your work up to the camera for 10 seconds so it can be read.
  • Use a laptop or desktop with Chrome or Edge in a quiet room. Aim the camera so it sees you and your paper or board.
